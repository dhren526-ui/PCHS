Tryout Information & Requirements

Eligibility

  1. Create/sign in to DragonFly Max.
  2. Complete the PCHS Swimming & Diving Application.
  3. Complete the PCHS Athletics Winter Tryout Intent Form.

Performance Standards

  • 50 yd freestyle under 45 seconds.
  • Legal 50 yd back, breast, and fly.
  • Block dive with head-first entry.
Tryouts take place at MAFC from 5:00–6:00 AM on October 29th & 30th. Final roster: Nov 3.
